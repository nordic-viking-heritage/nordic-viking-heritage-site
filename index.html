OUR MASTER MAP

Every step moves the fleet

Viking Voyage II turns everyday walking into a collective Atlantic crossing. The crew's combined steps determine how far the fleet advances, while each stage of the journey opens new stories, historical context and milestones.

The route begins in Greenland and heads toward the North American mainland before continuing south toward Florida. The map is not merely decoration — it is the visual logbook of the voyage.

THREE LAYERS — ONE EXPERIENCE

History, myth and our own saga

Ⅰ

HISTORY

Historically and archaeologically grounded material about Viking Age travel, seafaring, places and people. Where evidence is uncertain, we say so.

Ⅱ

MYTH & SAGA

Norse mythology gives the voyage its dramatic voice — ravens, gods, omens, storms and saga storytelling — clearly separated from documented history.

Ⅲ

OUR SAGA

The crew writes this layer together. Real steps, milestones, setbacks, records and shared moments become the continuing story of Viking Voyage II.

CHAPTER I

Across the North Atlantic

The opening chapter follows the fleet away from Greenland into the North Atlantic. Cold water, long distances and historic Norse horizons shape the visual language of the first stage.

As the crew moves south and west, the imagery will gradually change from ice, stone and grey-blue seas toward warmer coastal landscapes and ultimately the sunlight of Florida.

“Every step counts. Every Viking matters.”
