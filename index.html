HISTORY — DAY 10

THE NORSE IN NORTH AMERICA

Around AD 1000, Norse seafarers from Greenland reached lands west of the North Atlantic. The sagas preserve the names Helluland, Markland and Vinland, while archaeology gives us firm evidence at L’Anse aux Meadows in Newfoundland.

L’Anse aux Meadows is the authenticated Norse site in North America and anchors this chapter in evidence rather than legend.

THE SAGA MAY BE WILD. THE HISTORY MUST BE TRUE.

MYTH & SAGA — DAY 10

ÆGIR & RÁN — POWERS OF THE SEA

In Norse myth, Ægir is associated with the sea and with feasts for the gods. Rán, his wife, is a dangerous sea-being whose net is said to draw sailors beneath the waves.

This belongs to the mythic layer of Viking Voyage II — a story-world surrounding the crew’s real achievement, not a historical claim.
