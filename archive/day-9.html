CAPTAIN'S LOG · DAY 9

THE CROSSING CONTINUES.

Another Viking had joined the ship. Sixty-four sailors now faced west together as the fleet continued away from Greenland toward the North American mainland.

By the close of Day 9, the crew had recorded 9,904,699 steps — approximately 6,438 kilometres carried by sixty-four pairs of legs, one shared voyage and one stubborn fleet.

EVERY STEP MOVES THE SHIPS.
EVERY SAILOR MOVES THE FLEET.

— Captain Hákon
