CAPTAIN'S LOG · DAY 5

THE OCEAN OPENS BEFORE US.

Forty-eight sailors now carried the voyage west. By the fifth day, the fleet had reached 3,972,538 steps — approximately 2,582 kilometres from Greenland.

There was no road beneath us and no line across the sea. Only the growing distance behind the stern, the continent somewhere ahead and a crew learning what it meant to move as one.

FORTY-EIGHT SAILORS.
ONE SHIP. ONE CROSSING.

— Captain Hákon
