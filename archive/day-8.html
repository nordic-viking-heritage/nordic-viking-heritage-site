VOYAGEDAY 8
CAPTAIN'S LOG · DAY 8

WHEN ONE OAR RESTS, THE OTHERS PULL HARDER.

The fleet continued across the North Atlantic. Sixty-three sailors were aboard, and together the crew had carried Viking Voyage II to 8,765,483 steps — approximately 5,698 kilometres since Greenland.

But this day was remembered for something greater than the number.

ONE FLEET. ONE FAMILY.
NO VIKING GETS LEFT BEHIND.

— Captain Hákon
