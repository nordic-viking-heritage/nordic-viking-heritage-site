CAPTAIN'S LOG · DAY 7

NJÖRÐR HAS FILLED OUR SAILS.

The seventh day became one of the great surges of the crossing. The crew added 2,260,886 steps — roughly 1,470 kilometres — and the fleet pushed farther west across the North Atlantic.

Sixty-one sailors were now aboard. Greenland remained behind us. The North American mainland waited ahead.

THE CROSSING CONTINUES.
