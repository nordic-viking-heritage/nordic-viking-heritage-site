THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE SAGA

ANCIENT DNA

A research chamber for real archaeological individuals behind the Viking Age. Commercial databases may point us toward a sample, but only original research and primary scientific datasets may verify an entry.

ADNA-001

VK404 · Varnhem, Sweden

Research status: VERIFIED. VK404 belongs to the Varnhem/Skara material included in Margaryan et al., Population genomics of the Viking world. The study sequenced 442 ancient individuals from archaeological sites across Europe and Greenland and includes the Viking Age archaeological site at Varnhem in Skara municipality.

Evidence boundary: this record documents an archaeological individual and population-genomic research. It does not establish that VK404 is an ancestor of any Nordic Viking Heritage member, and commercial “historical match” results are never treated as proof of direct descent.

WHAT MUST NEVER BE LOST

THE CAPTAIN'S CODE

These are not decorative notes. They are the continuity laws of Viking Voyage II.

I · FLEET IDENTITY

Red-and-white sails. Coherent shields and oars.

II · FACES

Established participant faces are permanent. Never replace them with generic faces.

III · THREE WORLDS

HISTORY, MYTH & SAGA and OUR SAGA remain distinct.

IV · GEOGRAPHY

Movement follows verified steps and the locked route. Rest days do not move the map.

V · EVERY SAILOR MATTERS

Recognition is broader than leaderboard position.

VI · NAMES

Every Viking name is checked against the complete Ship's Roll and never reused.

VII · NIGHT WATCH

One Viking each night for one personal reason; rotate fairly.

VIII · PACER

Feed copy remains below 1,000 characters with safety margin.

IX · PUBLIC DESIGN

Approved HISTORY spacing and MYTH & SAGA contrast remain locked.

X · QUALITY

If continuity is uncertain, verify before publishing.

XI · OUR SAGA

Crew contributions may move the story; private correspondence remains private.

XII · PRIVACY

No private emails, rune readings, credentials or personal material enter this repository.

XIII · SOURCES

Historical claims retain sources and uncertainty boundaries.

XIV · DAILY RECORD

Every completed day receives a publication record; never invent a Pacer URL.

XV · ANCIENT DNA

Commercial databases are discovery tools only. Ancient DNA records require verification against original research or primary scientific data, and personal genetic results remain private.

XVI · ZERO-COST RULE

No feature, service, database or technical solution may make Nordic Viking Heritage dependent on a paid service. Cost target: 0 SEK. If a dependency becomes paid, replace it with a free solution rather than making payment a requirement.
