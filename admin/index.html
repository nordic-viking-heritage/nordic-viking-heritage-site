LOCKED CONTINUITY

PROJECT RULES

01 · FLEET IDENTITY

Viking Voyage II fleet sails are always red-and-white. Shields and oars must remain physically coherent.

02 · FACE CONTINUITY

Once a participant has an established face, always reuse it. Never replace it with a generic face. If the reference is unavailable, request it before creating the image.

03 · THREE STORY LAYERS

HISTORY = documented evidence. MYTH & SAGA = inherited stories and legend. OUR SAGA = the story created by the current crew. Never blur them.

04 · GEOGRAPHY

Movement follows verified steps and the locked route. Never invent fleet movement for visual convenience. A deliberate rest day does not move the map.

05 · EVERY SAILOR MATTERS

Crew recognition is broader than leaderboard position. Rotate attention fairly. Together we create our saga.

06 · NAMES ARE PERMANENT

Check the complete Ship's Roll before every new Viking naming. Previously used names are permanently blocked from reuse.

07 · NIGHT WATCH

From Day 27 onward, highlight one specific Viking each night for one personal reason. Normally no repeat until a reasonable rotation.

08 · PACER COPY

Feed posts must stay below Pacer's 1,000-character limit with a safety margin.

09 · VISUAL STRUCTURE

Approved public-page styling is locked. HISTORY keeps its established breathing room; MYTH & SAGA keeps its distinct darker chapter background. Daily updates change content, not these rules.

10 · QUALITY CONTROL

Do not publish uncertain continuity. Verify first. If a required fact, name, face or route position is uncertain, stop and check rather than improvise.

11 · PARTICIPANT-LED SAGA

When a crew member meaningfully changes the story, OUR SAGA may follow that contribution. Day 28 remains ashore because the Keeper asked for one more day; private correspondence stays private.

12 · PRIVACY BOUNDARY

Public project records may contain only material safe for the crew. Private emails, rune readings, personal details, credentials and unpublished correspondence never enter the public repository.

13 · SOURCE RECORD

Every historical claim used in the voyage should retain its source and its uncertainty boundary. Never turn a saga account or an interpretation into archaeological fact.

14 · DAILY POST RECORD

Every completed day receives an index of its published components and archive. Never invent a Pacer URL; use a textual post reference until a stable direct link is verified.
